Hello, my name is Ethan! I'm a Java full-stack software engineer with a background in data analytics and a strong interest in building systems that are clean under the hood, not just on the surface.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Texas at El Paso, with a concentration in Data Analytics and a minor in Mathematics. My work centers on the Java ecosystem — Spring Boot, REST APIs, JDBC, and JWT authentication — backed by PostgreSQL and MongoDB, with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the front end. I've built full-stack applications from schema design through deployment, including a recipe management platform with token-based auth and a terminal banking application handling account operations and transaction history. Outside of software, I produce music, which has given me a real appreciation for iteration and for knowing when something is actually finished. I'm always looking for the next problem worth solving.
